# Shutterlink privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026

Shutterlink is an iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch remote for cameras, published on the App Store as Shutterlink Remote by Apexmerce Solutions Private Limited, and an Android app published on Google Play under the same name. This policy covers both.

## We don't collect data

Shutterlink has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else, unless you choose to email us (see "If you contact us" below). The iPhone, iPad and Watch app has no third-party code; the Android app uses Google's ML Kit, which sends Google some diagnostics (see "On Android" below).

## What the app does on your device

- **Your camera.** The app talks only to your camera, over the camera's own Wi-Fi network (the Local Network permission). Live view, settings and pictures go between the camera and your iPhone or iPad and nowhere else.
- **Your camera's Wi-Fi.** If you let Shutterlink join the camera's Wi-Fi for you, the network name and password are kept on your device (the password in the Keychain) so the next connection is one tap. They aren't sent anywhere.
- **Camera (optional).** Only to scan the QR code on your camera's screen when you add a camera. The image isn't stored or sent anywhere.
- **Photos.** When you download a picture from the camera's card, the app saves it to your Photos library (the add-only Photos permission) or to Files. It doesn't read your library.
- **Scene suggestions.** If scene suggestions are on, the app looks at the live view image on your device, using Apple's on-device image recognition, to suggest settings (for example the night-sky helper when you point at the stars). The image isn't stored or sent anywhere, and nothing changes on the camera until you tap the suggestion. You can turn scene suggestions off in the app.
- **Location (optional).** If you turn on geotagging, the app notes your location while it's connected to the camera and adds it to the photos you save. If you've turned on geotagging, the app also uses that location to work out where the sun and moon are, for scene suggestions. The location stays on your device.
- **Settings.** The cameras you've connected to, your saved setups and your preferences are stored on your device. The Apple Watch app gets live view and camera status from the iPhone app through Apple's Watch connection, not over the internet.
- **Request log.** The app keeps a log of the commands it sends to the camera, to help with troubleshooting. It stays on your device unless you copy it yourself.
- **Report a problem (optional).** If you choose to, the app prepares an email (or something to share) with your description, the request log, the app version, your device model and system version, and your camera's model and connection details. It leaves out scanned QR codes, because they can contain the camera's Wi-Fi password. You see the email in your own email app and decide whether to send it; the app sends nothing by itself.

You can turn off the Local Network, Photos, Camera and Location permissions at any time in Settings on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes its data.

## On Android

Shutterlink Remote for Android works the same way, with these differences:

- **Google ML Kit.** Scene suggestions and the QR code scanner use Google's ML Kit, which runs on your phone: the live view and the code aren't sent anywhere. ML Kit itself sends Google some diagnostics (your phone's model and Android version, the app's version, an identifier for this installation that isn't meant to identify you, and performance figures), encrypted, which Google uses to keep ML Kit working and doesn't pass on to third parties. We don't receive it. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
- **QR code scanner.** It runs in Google Play services with its own camera screen, so Shutterlink never gets access to your camera.
- **Wi-Fi.** Joining the camera's Wi-Fi uses the Nearby devices permission (Android 13 and later) or location (Android 10 to 12). The network name and password stay on your phone, the password encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore.
- **Photos.** Downloaded pictures go to Pictures ▸ Shutterlink on your phone, RAW files to Download ▸ Shutterlink ▸ RAW.
- **Report a problem.** Works the same as on iPhone: nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
- **Purchases.** The Android app has no purchases yet.

## Purchases

Shutterlink Pro and its free trial are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked on this device.

## If you contact us

When you email us, including a problem report from the app, or sign up for the beta, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send: your message, and for a problem report the request log and the device, app and camera details listed above. Apexmerce Solutions Private Limited uses it only to answer you, fix the problem or send you a test invite. We don't sell it, share it or use it for marketing. We keep it only as long as we need it to help you, and we delete it if you ask us to (email the address below).
