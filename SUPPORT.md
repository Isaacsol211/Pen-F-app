# Shutterlink support

Shutterlink (Shutterlink Remote on the App Store) is a remote for Wi-Fi cameras on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, published by Apexmerce Solutions Private Limited.

We usually reply within a few days.

When something goes wrong with a camera, these help us most:

- your camera model and its firmware version
- your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version
- what you tapped and what happened
- the camera log: while connected, tap ⋯ ▸ Request log ▸ Copy log, then paste it into your message

## If the app can't find your camera

1. Turn on the camera's Wi-Fi for smartphone connection (for example Connection to Smartphone on an Olympus PEN-F).
2. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings ▸ Wi-Fi and join the camera's network. iOS may say the network has no internet; stay on it anyway.
3. Check that Local Network is on for Shutterlink in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network.
4. Open Shutterlink and tap Connect. Leave the brand on Automatic, or pick your camera's brand if Automatic doesn't find it.

Keep Shutterlink open on your iPhone while you shoot, including when you use the Apple Watch.

## Cameras

Shutterlink works with Olympus and OM System cameras with Wi-Fi remote shooting, Panasonic LUMIX cameras with Remote Shooting & View, Nikon Z bodies and DSLRs with Wi-Fi, Canon EOS bodies with CCAPI, and Sony bodies with Smart Remote Control (up to about 2019). What you can control depends on what each camera allows over Wi-Fi. If something doesn't work with your camera, please tell us.
